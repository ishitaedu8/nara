My name is Ishita Ghosh. I come from Falakata - a small village in West Bengal that most people have never heard of. I was an elite athlete. Powerlifting. Karate. Former World Ranker. National Champion. My body was everything. Then in early 2024 without warning - everything stopped. I spent 23 days in a semi-coma state. I could not speak. I could not eat alone. I could not brush my own teeth. My mother sat beside me every morning and placed the brush in my hand. She showed me how to hold it. How to move it. Like teaching a small child again. Doctors gave me a 5-year (1,825 days) prognosis. 5 years to recover. 5 years to be human again. I recovered in 1 year (12 months). I returned to Mumbai alone. Sat my final year college exams. Completed my graduation. In April 2026, I completed my graduation and received my degree. Nobody saw that coming. Least of all me. Then I watched my mother survive a silent 90% heart blockage that every device and every doctor nearly missed. Decades of stored survival stress. A body that never got permission to release what it was holding. That was the moment I understood why I was still here. I spent the next year reading everything about how the nervous system stores pain. How the body holds survival decisions made in childhood and keeps broadcasting them until someone shows it: the threat is over. I learned somatic healing. I learned breathwork. I learned how small daily habits build transformation so deep that even your ancestors benefit. Think of water heating on a stove. At 50 degrees - nothing changes. At 70 degrees - nothing changes. At 90 degrees - nothing changes. At 99 degrees - still nothing. But at 100 degrees? It does not just change. It transforms completely. It becomes something that rises. Most people quit at 99 degrees. They never see what was one single degree away. NARA is built for people who do not quit. Not a motivation app. Not a place to feel good for one day and disappear on day two. A place where you do the actual work. One mission. One day. One degree. Until you hit 100. I came from Falakata. I came back from a 23-day semi-coma. I came back from a 5-year (1,825 days) sentence. And I built this for you. Welcome to NARA. - Ishita, Solo Founder

## 1. Executive Summary
NARA is a somatic intelligence application designed for high performers and youngsters. It bridges the gap between biological survival and peak performance by identifying and "unfreezing" cellular memory through a gamified thermodynamic logic (The 100-Degree Principle).

## 2. Core Problem Statement
High performers frequently hit a "performance plateau" at 99 degrees. While the conscious mind (5%) wants to rise, the somatic nervous system (70%) is frozen in a past survival frequency. This biological conflict leads to burnout, overthinking, and systemic shutdown.

## 3. Target Audience
- **Primary:** High-performing achievers (Founders, Athletes, Executives).
- **Secondary:** Youngsters (Gen-Z/Alpha) facing early burnout and "freeze" states.
- **Tertiary:** Individuals recovering from neuropsychiatric or physical trauma looking for a logical recovery framework.

## 4. The "100-Degree" User Journey
- **The Diagnostic (Assessment):** A 10-question somatic frequency test mapping current baseline (Safety Seeker, Invisible Protector, Love Avoider, Abundance Blocker).
- **The Wisdom Layer:** Educational screens explaining the Iceberg Reality and the 1,825-day miracle trajectory.
- **The Ritual (Daily Mission):**

    - **Step 2 (Discharge):** Guided physical release (Shake, Cry, Rage, Stomp) with 60-second countdowns.
    - **Step 3 (Re-Code):** Written biological rule update (e.g., "I am safe to lead").
- **The Dashboard (Tracker):** A 90-day progress bar tracking "Degrees of Heat" toward transformation.
